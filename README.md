# PaymentSave PAX Solution
![CI](https://github.com/devnied/EMV-NFC-Paycard-Enrollment/workflows/CI/badge.svg) [![Maven Central](https://maven-badges.herokuapp.com/maven-central/com.github.devnied.emvnfccard/library/badge.svg?style=flat)](https://maven-badges.herokuapp.com/maven-central/com.github.devnied.emvnfccard/library) [![License](http://img.shields.io/:license-apache-blue.svg)](http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html)
PaymentSavePax is a versatile and user-friendly app designed to simplify the process of accepting payments for businesses and individuals. With seamless integration and robust security features, PaymentPro provides a hassle-free way to manage transactions.

## Features

- Accept Various Payment Methods: Allow your customers to pay using credit/debit cards, mobile wallets, and other popular payment methods.
- Invoice Generation: Create and send professional invoices to clients with detailed breakdowns of products or services rendered.
- Real-time Transaction Monitoring: Keep track of all transactions in real-time, providing transparency and aiding in financial management.
- Security First: Utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols to ensure the safety of sensitive payment information.
- Customizable Payment Settings: Tailor the app to your business needs with customizable settings for taxes, discounts, and currency preferences.
- Multi-platform Compatibility: Access PaymentPro on mobile devices and desktops, providing flexibility for users.
- Instant Payment Notifications: Receive instant notifications for successful transactions, enabling prompt order fulfillment or service delivery.


Whether you run a small business, freelance, or sell products online, PaymentPro empowers you to handle transactions effortlessly, creating a secure and convenient payment experience for both you and your customers.
